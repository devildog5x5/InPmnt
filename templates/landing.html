Unpaid invoices cost time before they cost cash

A small shop usually already knows who owes what. The drag is remembering to ask, wording it so it still sounds like you, and noticing when the money finally lands.

  • The follow-up eats the evening

    Checking a notebook, rewriting the same email, and wondering whether you already asked this week is unpaid work on top of the unpaid invoice.

  • The ask feels awkward

    InvoicePay starts from templates you can edit: a friendly nudge, a due-today note, an overdue follow-up, and a final notice. The words stay yours.

  • Cash shows up late

    The dashboard lists the open balance and sorts it into current, 1–30, 31–60, and 60+ days, so you can see which invoices have been quiet the longest.

A plain piece of arithmetic

If one reminder gets one $200 invoice paid a week sooner, that single invoice is more than three years of InvoicePay at $4.99 a month. $4.99 × 36 months is $179.64. This is not a survey. It is $200 compared with the subscription.

How it works

Four steps from a client and an invoice to a recorded payment.

1. Add the client and the invoice

Save the name, email, and phone, then the invoice number, amount, and due date.

2. Pick the reminder days

The starting schedule is 3 days before due, the due date, then 3, 7, and 14 days after. You can change those offsets.

3. Send the note you wrote

Email goes out when Resend or SMTP is connected. SMS templates unlock on the paid plan. A phone carrier is not connected yet, so texts are kept in the reminder log.

4. Mark it paid

Record a partial or full payment. When the balance is gone, reminders that are still queued stop.

Questions before you start

Do I need a credit card to start?
No. The 14-day trial opens with your name, email, and a password. A card is required only when you choose monthly or yearly billing.
What does InvoicePay cost after the trial?
InvoicePay is $4.99 a month or $49.99 a year. Both include unlimited open invoices plus email reminders and SMS templates. The yearly price is $9.89 less than twelve months at $4.99.
How do the reminders actually go out?
You write the message. InvoicePay queues it for the days you pick. Email sends when you connect Resend or SMTP. SMS templates are included on the paid plan. A phone carrier is not connected in this version, so those texts are saved in your reminder log.
Is there a limit on open invoices?
The 14-day trial allows 40 open invoices. Monthly and yearly plans remove that cap. Queued reminders stop once the invoice is paid.
